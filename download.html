HEAVEN’S PILLAR is available for Sega Saturn as well as PC!
Built from the ground up for enthusiasts, this native homebrew version runs on original hardware (using an ODE, flash cartridge, or burned disc where supported) and compatible emulators.

Can’t Wait to Start?

HEAVEN’S PILLAR is a fast-paced co-op beat 'em up with the depth and precision of a fighting game. Battle across a world on the brink of collapse as two fallen gods fight to reclaim their stolen divinity. Play instantly in your browser, on PC, or on the Sega Saturn.
