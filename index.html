The Eight Pillars Are Crumbling

HEAVEN’S PILLAR is a fast-paced co-op beat 'em up with the depth and precision of a fighting game. Battle across a world on the brink of collapse as two fallen gods fight to reclaim their stolen divinity. Play instantly in your browser, on PC, or on the Sega Saturn.

The Fight of Your Life

Chain together launchers, juggles, throws, and special moves in combat inspired by arcade classics. Every encounter rewards timing, positioning, and creativity, giving you the freedom to discover your own combat style.

Can't Go It Alone?

Fight side by side in local or online co-op. Extend each other’s combos, cover your partner when the pressure builds, and tear through enemies together. Whether you’re playing on the couch or across the internet, teamwork is always your greatest weapon.

Built for the Sega Saturn

The demo is available for modern PCs and the Sega Saturn. Experience HEAVEN’S PILLAR on original hardware or your favorite emulator, built specifically for the iconic, cult-classic 32-bit console.
